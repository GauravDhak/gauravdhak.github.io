About

Building Digital Systems One Clock Cycle at a Time

I am a B.Tech graduate in Electronics System Engineering from NIELIT Aurangabad and currently work as a Project Associate at the National Institute of Technology Goa.

My work focuses on FPGA-based hardware acceleration, RTL design, digital system implementation, and VLSI design flow.

I enjoy transforming algorithms into efficient hardware architectures through Verilog, FPGA prototyping, and hardware-software co-design.
